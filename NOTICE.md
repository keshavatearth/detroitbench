unlicensed; it is not redistributed here. The chapter's interaction order was
cross-checked against a recorded playthrough and public walkthroughs listed in
`SOURCES.md`.

three reference screenshots used for that layout come from a public Imgur album
posted on r/DetroitBecomeHuman (see `references/hostage-flowchart/provenance.json`)
and are kept only as layout references, not under the MIT licence.
