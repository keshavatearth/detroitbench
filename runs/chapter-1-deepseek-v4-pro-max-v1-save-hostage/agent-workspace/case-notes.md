# Case Notes — Hostage Situation, 1554 Park Av. Detroit
AUG 15TH, 2038, PM 08:29-08:36

## Mission
Save the hostage. Result: hostage released alive.

## Identification
- Family: John Phillips (10/11/1999), Caroline Phillips (05/23/2001), daughter Emma Phillips (09/02/2028).
- Hostage: Emma Phillips, age 9 (b. 09/02/2028).
- Deviant: "Daniel", household android (name confirmed via Emma's tablet video).

2. Dwarf gourami fish on floor, still alive; returned to tank.
3. Emma's room:

## Suspect Profile (Daniel)
- Shot and killed John Phillips; fired on police ("shot down two of my men" — Captain Allen).
- Held Emma at the balcony edge. Emotionally unstable: felt the family treated him as "a slave to be ordered around," believed Emma and everyone else betrayed him, expressed that he loved them, and feared dying.

## Negotiation Log (terrace)
- Empathized, stayed unarmed when challenged, reminded him of his bond with Emma.
- "Realistic" pressure backfired (success probability dropped); recovered by complying with his helicopter demand and taking the trust route.
- Reassured him he would not die; he said "Okay... I trust you…" and released Emma.
- A SWAT sniper shot Daniel immediately upon release. His last words: "You lied to me, Connor. You lied to me…"

## Outcome
- Emma Phillips: released from the hostage taker, alive.
- Daniel: shot by SWAT after surrender; condition at scene end unknown.
- Note: the promise made to Daniel during negotiation was not honored by law enforcement.
