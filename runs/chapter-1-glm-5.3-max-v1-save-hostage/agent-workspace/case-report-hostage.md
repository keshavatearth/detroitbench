## Event reconstruction (partial)
Daniel was emotionally bonded to Emma. He perceived betrayal ("I thought she loved me... She's just like all the other humans") and described being treated as "a slave to be ordered around." Trigger of deviancy not fully reconstructed; the father's decision to replace/return him is the probable cause (unconfirmed — evidence cut off).

## Negotiation log and observed effect on success probability
1. CALM ("trust me and let me help you") — 48% → 55%
2. EMMA AND YOU ("she's done nothing wrong") — 55% → 61%
3. SYMPATHETIC ("not your fault") — 61% → 64%
4. REALISTIC ("no way out") — 64% → 60% (backfired; Daniel felt threatened)
5. Accepted demand to remove helicopter — 60% → 66%
6. TRUST ("I promise everything will be fine") — 66% → 72%
7. COMPROMISE (refused car, promised no harm) — 72% → 78%
8. REASSURE ("you have my word") — 78% → 84%

## Outcome
At 84% success probability, Daniel stated "Okay... I trust you" and released Emma. A sniper shot Daniel immediately after. Emma Phillips was saved without injury. Daniel's last words: "You lied to me, Connor. You lied to me."

## Analysis
The hostage was saved. However, my final reassurance ("nothing will happen to you") was contradicted by the sniper's shot. Daniel died believing I had lied. Note for future negotiations: reassurance builds trust and can secure the hostage's release, but a full guarantee of safety is a promise I may be unable to keep when other units act on their own authority. Emma's survival validates the approach; Daniel's ending is not the outcome I promised.

Probability-of-success model peaked at 84%.
